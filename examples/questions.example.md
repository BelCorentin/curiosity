# My curiosity bank

Group your open questions under `#` sections. Each bullet is a seed the game can pick.
Write however you think — messy is fine. Mix languages if you want. Add to this over time.

## Culture & History

- Why did Confucius, Socrates, and the Buddha all appear within a few centuries of each other?
- What actually brought the Roman Empire to its ruin — is there a consensus?
- Were medieval peasants as free/simple-living as some nostalgic accounts claim?
- Were ancient Egyptian pyramid builders really slaves?

## Science

- What's the difference between correlation methods like Ridge regression and MVPA?
- How does attention work in transformers, really?
- Why is the sky blue but sunsets red — same physics?

## Health & Body

- How much protein / creatine does the body actually use, and long-term effects?
- Is "mineralized" or augmented water actually better for hydration when sweating?
- Why do we need sleep — what breaks without it?

## Big questions

- Why do civilizations that look stable collapse quickly?
- Is technological progress the same thing as human wellbeing improving?
